Data Deletion

Last updated: October 22, 2026

BeatNest’s current website stores its local profile and preferences in this browser’s local storage. The site does not currently provide server-side BeatNest accounts. Use the button below on the same browser and website origin to remove BeatNest’s local data from this browser.

This will remove local BeatNest profile data, selected languages, liked songs, and playlists from this browser. This action cannot be undone. It does not delete YouTube/Google data or data stored by third-party services.

Manual deletion

You can also remove the site’s local storage using your browser’s site-data settings. Clearing all browser data may affect other sites as well.

Requests and questions

For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning information outside your browser, contact the site operator using the contact details on the Contact page. Information controlled by YouTube/Google must be managed through their own services and policies.

BeatNest is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
