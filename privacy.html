Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BeatNest (“BeatNest,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit our music-discovery website. Please review it before using the service.

1. How BeatNest uses YouTube

BeatNest uses the YouTube Data API to help discover and organize publicly available music videos and related metadata, such as video titles, channel names, thumbnails, and video IDs. Music playback is provided through the YouTube embedded player. BeatNest does not claim ownership of videos hosted on YouTube.

Your use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

2. Information stored on your device

BeatNest currently stores preferences and library data in your browser’s local storage. Depending on how you use the site, this may include your selected languages, liked video IDs, playlist names and contents, display name, email address, and a locally generated password hash for the site’s local sign-in feature. This local sign-in feature is not Google Account sign-in. The website’s current client-side implementation stores this information in your browser rather than sending it to a BeatNest account server. Do not reuse a password from another service.

Music catalog metadata is loaded from the website’s published catalog files. The site also loads YouTube’s embedded player and may load website hosting/analytics resources when configured. Those third-party services may process technical information under their own policies.

3. YouTube and Google privacy

When you interact with an embedded YouTube player, YouTube/Google may collect information as described in their policies. Please review the Google Privacy Policy. BeatNest does not control YouTube’s data practices.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

BeatNest itself uses browser local storage for preferences and library data. The embedded YouTube player and other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own settings and policies.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local BeatNest data remains in your browser until you clear it or remove it using the Data Deletion page. Clearing browser storage may remove your local profile, preferences, likes, and playlists. This does not delete information held by YouTube or other third parties.

6. Children and age

BeatNest is a general music-discovery website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information should contact the site operator using the contact method below.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the website or its data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
